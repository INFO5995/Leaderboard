Bonus Rankings

Vulnerability Records

This leaderboard only lists validated zero-day vulnerabilities. Confirmed duplicates of non-public reports are included as zero-day. Originality is shown separately so first disclosures and zero-day duplicates are not conflated. Officially triaged entries that are not finally confirmed are marked with * and receive 0.5 provisional credit. Project names and report identifiers are temporarily withheld.

Platform Course

Duplicate entries require a countable duplicate/original status. Official platform or vendor triage counts provisionally at 0.5 credit only when it is more than submission intake or generic pending review. Retesting, Resolved, Closed, fixed, or otherwise finally confirmed entries count at full credit. New, pending review, needs-more-info, under-review, Informative, Informational, or status-hidden duplicates are withheld until confirmed.
